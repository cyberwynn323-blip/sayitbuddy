    How Say It Buddy works

    • All 50 words are free forever — the full word set from Say It & Color It! by Luke Wynn. Nothing about learning is ever locked.
    • Hear It plays the word in a recorded man's voice. Say It plays the word once first, then opens the microphone and listens for your child to say it — and the “I said it!” button always works if a microphone is blocked or hears nothing. Trying always earns the star; there are no wrong answers.
    • Calm by design: no flashing, no timers, no punishment, no game over, no ads, no accounts, no subscription.
    • Progress stays on this device: stars, learned words, your child's pet, and the outfits unlock are saved only in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere or collected.
    • The only paid extra is the Buddy Outfits Pack above — $4.99 one time, for grown-ups only, in this Grown-ups area. Children never see a buy button.
